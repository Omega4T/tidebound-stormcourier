# Stormcourier visual direction

Owner selected: sailor board game with printed map, boat pieces, ink labels and logbook panels. Antislop mode: during, session choice; no global preference changed.

Design Read: a playable sailing board game for desktop keyboard/mouse players, using printed cartography and physical game-piece language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1 for interface. Essential sailing/hazard animation remains gameplay; nonessential menu motion is absent and reduced motion freezes decorative water/rain.

## Identity and reason

- Warm ivory paper (#eee5ca) and deep ink (#173d4b) create the material contrast of a sea chart; one dark rust accent (#9c3c28) marks the primary action and danger.
- Sea is pale blue-grey, land is paper cutout, shoreline/reef hatching denotes impassable terrain. Pattern is cartographic meaning, not a full-page decorative grid.
- Georgia bold serif is the game-box title voice, chosen for map-title character and local/offline availability. Arial is the readable small-label face; Courier is restricted to navigation annotations/logbook coordinates.
- The title behaves as an opened board-game box: compact masthead, asymmetrical title/instructions left, authored archipelago right. No hero badge, floating stats, glow, glass, or stock marketing section.
- Gameplay instruments form a paper ledger across the top, freeing the board for navigation. The most important content is next objective, then hull and storm time; secondary values recede.
- Harbor decisions form a supply ledger rather than three marketing cards. Prices, consequences and disabled states are visible; only the next-departure button is emphasized.
- Result is a signed-off voyage sheet showing actual achieved progress, a clear outcome and replay. No invented success metrics, testimonials or status badges.
- Genuine map station numbers are the three implemented region names. Fictional geography is authored game content, not a claim of real-world survey accuracy.
- The same player boat is rendered as a wooden token in menu and actual play. No new logo, avatar, external assets or libraries.
- Flat surfaces and offset ink lines express paper/game pieces. Shadows used only on raised ship and open logbook panel to distinguish world/interaction planes.

## Layout and scope

Logical world remains1280x720 to preserve physics. Desktop is the full board. Compact screens receive an explicit readable title/controls layout rather than a squeezed desktop menu. The bounty requires keyboard/mouse desktop play; narrow portrait should communicate that requirement honestly and not claim touch gameplay. All available menu controls remain44px touch targets; avoid overflow. Tablet/landscape board may scale, with a compact title layout.

## Content and interactions

Keep actual3-region,6-signal campaign, resource economy, two real route contracts, upgrade effects and score. Screenshots come from actual title/play/dock/result through ordinary controls. Never fabricate a state for image composition. No UI-driven gameplay balance changes in this pass.

Keyboard: Tab follows native controls, Enter/Space activate buttons, C chart, P/Escape pause or close chart, R restart, M sound. Do not hijack Tab. Focus visible; dialogs named and keyboard reachable. Muted palette still must meet measured contrast. No tracking, fetch, third-party fonts or online imagery. All labels use actual game state.
